1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Space Maths ("we", "us", "our") handles personal data when you use the Space Maths app.

Space Maths is operated from Switzerland.

For privacy questions or support requests please contact: support@spacemaths.app

2. The short version

  • The app does not require users to create an account.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses or contact details unless you contact us directly.
  • Your scores, progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not display third-party advertising.
  • You can delete your data at any time by resetting the app or uninstalling it.

3. What data we handle

Subscription information

If you purchase a subscription, payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive your payment card information.

If we use RevenueCat, RevenueCat may receive anonymous user identifiers, subscription status information and purchase entitlement data in order to manage subscriptions.

Data stored on your device

The following information may be stored locally on your device:

  • Profile name
  • Profile picture
  • Scores
  • Quiz history
  • Learning progress
  • App settings and preferences

We do not store this information on our own servers.

Technical information

Apple and RevenueCat may process technical information such as IP addresses and device information for security, fraud prevention and service operation purposes.

What we do not collect

  • Precise location data
  • Contact lists
  • Health information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Cross-app tracking information

If you choose a profile photograph, the app may request access to your photo library solely for that purpose.

4. Why we use data

  • To provide app functionality.
  • To verify subscriptions.
  • To deliver paid content.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To maintain security and prevent misuse.

5. Third-party services

  • Apple App Store
  • RevenueCat

These providers operate under their own privacy policies.

Some data may be processed outside Switzerland, the United Kingdom, Australia or the European Economic Area using appropriate legal safeguards.

6. Data retention

We do not maintain personal learning records on our servers.

Data stored on your device remains there until you remove it, reset the app or uninstall the app.

Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own retention policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, including under Swiss, UK, EU and Australian privacy legislation, you may have rights to:

  • Access personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion
  • Restrict processing
  • Object to processing
  • Request a copy of your information

You may contact us at: support@spacemaths.app

8. Children

Space Maths is designed for school-aged students.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and, where appropriate, delete the information.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures, encrypted connections and trusted service providers to protect information.

No internet-based service can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

The date shown at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
