Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: Space Math does not collect, share or sell any personal information. There are no ads, no accounts, no sign-in and no tracking. Everything your child does in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

Space Math (also shown as "Astro Math" in some languages) is an educational app for young children, made by an independent developer. If you have a question about this policy, email support.acemath@proton.me.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or anything else. The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or social features, and it does not use any third-party tracking tools.

What is saved on the device

So that your child can carry on where they left off, some things are saved only on the iPhone or iPad:

  • the first name a grown-up types in the Parent area (optional – you can leave it blank)
  • finished pages, missions, trophies and Moon badges
  • your child's drawings on the workbook pages
  • settings such as language, sound and the daily time limit

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your own device backup (iCloud or computer), which is controlled by you and Apple. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Pictures and fonts from the internet

To keep the download small, the workbook pictures and the letter styles (fonts) are downloaded when needed from Google's servers (Google Cloud Storage and Google Fonts). As with visiting any website, these servers receive the device's internet (IP) address so they can send the files back. No name, progress, drawings or other information is sent. You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

The first pages of every section are free. A grown-up can unlock the whole workbook with an optional monthly or yearly subscription (new subscribers get a free trial). Subscribing, renewals, cancelling and refunds are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID; we do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple, on the device, whether a subscription is active. The subscription screen is protected by a grown-up question.

Printing

When a grown-up prints a page, the app creates the printable file on the device and hands it to Apple's print and share options. We never receive it. Printing is protected by a grown-up question.

Children's privacy

Space Math is made for children and is designed to meet the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Apple's App Store rules for the Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The Parent area is protected by a grown-up question, and the app contains no links to websites and no ads. The optional purchase is also behind the grown-up question.

Your rights and choices

Because we hold no data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You stay in control on the device: change or remove the child's name in the Parent area at any time, or delete the app to remove everything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top, and update the copy inside the app.

Contact

Email support.acemath@proton.me. We aim to reply within a few working days.
